The balance

The answer isn't a brick phone

Children don't want to be cut off from their friends and the world — and they shouldn't have to be. A smartphone is how they find their way, look things up and learn. The goal isn't to take that away; it's to give them today's world while keeping them safe in it.

Just a few examples of the apps and everyday functionality a modern phone gives a child — the very things an older "brick" phone simply doesn't have:

Finding their way

Maps to get to school, a friend's house or the right bus — the everyday independence that a basic phone can't give them.

Looking things up

Homework, curiosity, keeping up with the world — a safely filtered web is one of the main ways children learn today.

Learning and growing

Educational apps like Duolingo, reading and revision tools — the good things a locked‑down phone would shut out along with the bad.

A basic "brick" phone keeps children safe by cutting them off. Tethar does the opposite: it keeps everything good about a modern smartphone, and quietly removes what isn't — so the balance tips towards access and safety, not one at the cost of the other.

Good questions

Frequently asked

Why is Tethar only on Android?
We build only for Android so we can protect it as fully as possible — on Android a parental‑control app can manage which apps run, filter the web through our own browser and work quietly in the background. iPhone isn't supported yet, though iOS is on our roadmap.
Will my child know it's installed?
Tethar is designed to be transparent, not sneaky. It keeps the phone looking and feeling normal rather than hiding — we believe healthy digital habits are built on trust, and you decide how much to involve your child.
How is my family's data handled?
Data integrity is of the utmost importance to us. Data is only ever visible to you, and never sold. We collect the minimum needed to keep your child safe.
Can two parents manage the same child?
Yes. Co-parenting is built in — invite another parent or guardian to share access to your dashboard so you're both kept in the loop.
How many children can I add?
As many as you like. Every plan includes unlimited children at one price — add each child and manage them all from a single parent dashboard.
What happens after the 30 days?
You get 30 days free. On day 31, billing begins via Google Play — £5 a month or £47.50 a year — unless you cancel first. You can cancel any time in Google Play, in two taps, and we never see your card.
How do I cancel?
Cancel any time in Google Play, in two taps — the same place you signed up. Billing runs through Google Play, so we never see your card and there's nothing to phone or email about.
