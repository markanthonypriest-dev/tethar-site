Cookie Policy

Last updated: 17 July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Tethar (Halo Apps Ltd) uses cookies and similar technologies on www.tethar.com.

At the moment, this website sets no advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we'll ask for your consent first through the cookie banner — and "Reject" will always be as easy as "Accept".

What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website can store on your device. "Similar technologies" includes your browser's local storage. Some are essential to make a site work; others are optional, such as those used to measure how a site is used.

What we use today

We do not currently use any optional or tracking cookies. We use a small amount of essential local storage in your browser purely to make features work:

  • Your cookie choice — so we don't ask again on every visit.
  • Interactive demo — remembers the settings you toggle while trying the on-page demo.
  • Waitlist — briefly notes that you've signed up, so the form behaves sensibly.

These stay on your device, are not used to track you across other websites, and are not shared for advertising.

Optional cookies (if we add them)

We may later use privacy-friendly analytics to understand which pages are useful. If we do, those cookies will be listed here and will only be set after you agree through the banner. Until you accept, they stay switched off.

Managing your choices

When optional cookies are in use, you can change your mind at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. You can also clear or block cookies and local storage through your browser's settings.

Changes

We'll update this page whenever our use of cookies changes, and update the "last updated" date above.
